# Code of Conduct

minelog is for people who are stuck and want help. The project should feel that way too.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are players, not programmers.
- Assume good faith and ask before you assume the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep it in English so everyone can follow along.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Sexual or violent content
- Sharing other people's private information
- Spam and self promotion unrelated to the project

## What happens

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people
who break these rules. If you see something wrong, email **report@minelog.org**. We will
keep the report private.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space that belongs to
the project.
